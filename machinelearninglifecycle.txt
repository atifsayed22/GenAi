4. EDA (Exploratory Data Analysis)  : EDA primarily stands for Exploratory Data Analysis, a critical

    characteristics, often using visual methods, before applying formal modeling.  Developed by John 
    Tukey in the 1970s, this open-ended approach helps identify patterns, anomalies, and relationships,
     guiding hypothesis generation and ensuring data quality for subsequent machine learning or 
     statistical analysis
